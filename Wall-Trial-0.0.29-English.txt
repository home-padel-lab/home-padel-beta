Home Padel Lab 0.0.29 - wall response trial

Wall rebound is now 0.75 instead of 0.55. Racket power, racket compression,
floor bounce, friction, gravity, drag, spin and court dimensions are unchanged.
This is an experimental comparison, not certified realistic padel physics.

On first launch, the old untouched 0.55 setting upgrades to 0.75. Custom wall
settings remain unchanged. Grip, room placement and reports are preserved.
The previous wall preference stays saved separately.

To compare: use Medium singles, turn off the ball machine and bot, then try
gentle upward shots, direct wall shots and back-wall counters with similar
swings. Afterwards try a rally with the bot. Feedback is recorded as before.

To return to the previous response: open the left-controller Menu button,
choose Court & physics, then Bounce & racket power > Wall bounce, and set it
to 0.55. Save/close the menu. This deliberately restored value is respected
on subsequent launches. Do not reset all physics to change just the wall.

Also includes the already prepared 0.0.28 changes: no racket swing sound,
a room-anchored options menu, and joystick court positioning. On the court
placement screen: left stick moves, right stick rotates, trigger is fine
adjustment, X accepts and Y cancels. Keep the physical play area clear;
virtual repositioning does not replace the Quest boundary.

This update does not claim to resolve dizziness. Stop if symptoms return.
