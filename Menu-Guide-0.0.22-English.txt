HOME PADEL LAB 0.0.22 — MENU GUIDE
English beta for Quest 3 / 3S. Actual headset testing is still needed.

WHAT CHANGED
Four sections replace the growing flat options list. Long screens show five
rows at a time; use up/down to see more. Y returns to the previous screen and
remembers the highlighted row. Your physics, racket fit and ball fit are kept.

CONTROLS — LEFT CONTROLLER, EVEN IF YOUR RACKET IS LEFT-HANDED
Menu (three lines): open options / save all changes and close.
Stick up/down: select a row. Stick left/right: change its value.
Push firmly (75%); release towards centre for a single step. Holding repeats.
X: the action written at the bottom of the screen.
Y: back, or cancel a pending restore/import.
Left index trigger: finer steps for racket fit, ball fit and feel controls.
On numeric rows, X resets ONLY that selected value to its default; the footer
explicitly says so. Restoring a whole fit, all physics or the court, or loading
a shared setup, requires a SECOND X press. Y or changing row cancels it.

START HERE
1. Equipment fit > Racket fit: choose your racket hand. Adjust RED / GREEN /
   BLUE position axes using the matching live arrows. Scroll down for face
   tilt, handle twist and side lean. Save and test grip closes with no ball.
2. Equipment fit > Ball in hand: adjust the separate FREE-hand ball position.
   Save and try a drop. Fully release the trigger, then squeeze it to hold a
   ball. Release while still to drop; move the hand to toss. No launch boost.
3. Play & training > Ball machine: choose Slow straight shot, then Launch one
   ball. The menu closes and ONE ball launches after two seconds. Select
   Start practice for repeated balls after the chosen interval instead.

PLAY & TRAINING
Play against the bot: select Easy / Medium / Advanced; scroll to START selected
mode. Default mode is BOT when opened from this entry. Starting enables the bot
but does NOT serve. FREE-hand X (left) / A (right) serves one ball. Y / B stops
the bot. Easy mostly stays near the centre; Medium and Advanced vary placement.
The mode selector can switch to MACHINE; START alone does not enable auto-feed.
Ball machine: 15 shot types, interval, repeat-one or cycle-all mode, separate
single-ball and auto-feed actions. An infeasible drill is blocked with a message.
Free practice: closes with bot and machine OFF. Use the FREE-hand index trigger
to hold, drop or toss a ball. The trigger is disabled during automatic feeding.
Controller help: four short explanations; it never starts a mode or changes fit.

COURT & PHYSICS
Court size: length / width in metres. Ball speed is NOT scaled with court size.
Bounce & racket power: wall bounce, floor bounce and racket power. Lower wall
bounce loses more energy. Change one value at a time, then Test with ball machine.
Advanced spin & friction: floor grip, wall grip, racket spin grip, spin in flight.
These remain experimental tuning values, not measured padel material constants.
Restore physics defaults resets all SEVEN physics values, not your personal fit.
No automatic new court presets, racket dimensions or speed-scaling model are
introduced in this build. Default wall response remains 0.55, racket power 0.45.

REPORTS & SHARING
Recording, tests & feedback: Auto-save game data records normal play locally.
Run calibration: five fixed tests; watch rather than hit. Opening Menu cancels.
Your feedback: choose a rating, then Save feedback. Export test report flushes
the local report; a PC/USB is needed to retrieve it. There is no auto-upload.
Share or load a setup: choose the scope. Screenshot the HP1 code and send it
with your app version. Export JSON is optional. Loading needs SharedSetup.json
in /sdcard/Android/data/com.homepadel.lab/files and a second X confirmation.
HP1 scopes contain racket fit and/or physics, court and machine settings only.
Ball-in-hand fit and bot difficulty are NOT included in this legacy setup code.
No camera image, audio or room scan is recorded. Send reports only voluntarily.

IMPORTANT
Opening Menu stops BOTH bot and machine and cancels pending launches. Closing
does not automatically resume training: deliberately start/serve again.
Virtual walls do not prove that your room is safe. Keep the Quest boundary on,
clear furniture and use wrist straps. Move only within the safe cleared area.
This build has no obstacle scan or multiplayer.

UPDATE / INSTALL
Install HomePadel-0.0.22-English.apk with your existing APK installer, or with
Android Platform Tools: adb install -r HomePadel-0.0.22-English.apk
Enable developer mode, connect a USB DATA cable and allow USB debugging inside
the headset. An update keeps app settings and reports; do NOT uninstall first.
Open Home Padel Lab in the sideloaded / Unknown Sources library.
