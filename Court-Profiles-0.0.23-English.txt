HOME PADEL LAB 0.0.23 — COURT SIZE & MATCH FORMAT

This version adds court layouts; it is NOT a multiplayer release.
The purchased racket, personal fit, floor/wall response and player racket-speed
curve are unchanged. Net: 0.78 m; side glass: 1.40 m; back glass: 2.00 m.

HOW TO SELECT
Press LEFT Menu (three lines) > Court & physics > Court size.
Up/down selects a row; left/right changes the selected value.
Use a deliberate stick movement beyond 75%; return the stick to centre between
steps. X advances a preset or toggles match format. Y goes back.

1. Singles size preset: Compact, Medium or Large.
2. Match format: SINGLES or DOUBLES layout.
3. Playing court (width x length): calculated, read-only dimensions.
4-5. Custom singles width/length: optional manual adjustments to the BASE size.
6. Reset to Compact singles: requires X twice; Y cancels.
7. Save and play: saves and closes. Bot and machine remain OFF until started.

                     SINGLES             DOUBLES
Compact              2.4 x 6.0 m         4.8 x 9.6 m
Medium               3.0 x 7.0 m         6.0 x 12.0 m
Large                5.0 x 10.0 m       10.0 x 20.0 m
All dimensions above are WIDTH x LENGTH.
Doubles width = twice the selected singles width.
Doubles length = twice its NEW width (fixed length:width ratio of 2:1).
Returning to Singles restores the base singles size, including a custom length.
Changing custom singles length does not change doubles length: its ratio is fixed.

UPDATING
Install HomePadel-0.0.23-English.apk without uninstalling the existing app.
Existing dimensions are preserved exactly and shown as Custom (saved singles)
if they do not match a named preset. This includes the previous 2.4 x 6.5 m court.
Selecting Compact explicitly changes that base size to 2.4 x 6.0 m.
New installations start in Compact Singles. Previous fit, feel and reports stay.

SHARING
New setup codes use HP2 so a Physics setup can restore the singles base size and
doubles flag, not just the outer dimensions. HP1 codes and old JSON still import
in this version; older versions cannot import HP2. Racket-only imports still keep
the receiving player's court and physics. Ball fit and bot level are NOT included.
Reports are local; there is no automatic upload.

TRAINING
Play & training > Play against the bot > choose difficulty > START.
FREE-hand X/A serves; Y/B stops the bot. The usual free-hand trigger hold/drop/toss
is available in free practice and when the bot is ready for a serve.
Play & training > Ball machine: choose a drill, then Start practice or Launch one
ball. Opening Menu stops both modes; closing alone does not restart them.
Machine/bot planning now supports the larger courts. Launch trajectories on
layouts longer than 8 m are recalculated to remain feasible, including faster
machine launches where needed. This does NOT scale your swing, gravity, drag,
spin, restitutions or friction. Compact/saved courts up to 8 m retain old plans.

SAFETY AND LIMITS
Virtual court size does not measure safe real room space. Keep the Quest boundary
active, use wrist straps and clear arm/controller reach. Large or doubles layouts
do not imply that your room is large enough to move safely across the whole court.
No furniture scan, teleport locomotion, second bot, doubles scoring or networking.
Automated planning tests do not certify realistic feel or headset performance.
