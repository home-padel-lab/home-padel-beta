HOME PADEL LAB 0.0.28 - COURT PLACEMENT AND QUIET RACKET MOVEMENT

Local APK. Not yet installed on Quest or published on GitHub.

Changes
- Racket movement / whoosh sound removed completely from gameplay and preview.
  Real racket/ball impacts, floor, glass, net, rolling and UI sounds remain.
- The options panel stays at its physical-room anchor when court size or
  placement changes. It no longer gets left behind in the previous court frame.
- Dedicated joystick court placement screen; training is paused while editing.
- Existing grip, bounce, racket power, ball flight and court sizes are preserved.

How to place the court
1. Clear a safe space and keep your Quest boundary enabled. Virtual walls do
   NOT detect or remove real furniture. Stand still while moving the court.
2. LEFT Menu > Court & physics > Place court in your room.
3. Centre BOTH joysticks before starting.
4. LEFT joystick: slide the virtual court left/right or nearer/farther.
   Directions follow where you are looking; there is no up/down height change.
5. RIGHT joystick left/right: turn the virtual court around your current feet.
6. Hold the LEFT index trigger for smaller, slower position/angle adjustments.
7. X: KEEP placement and return to the previous menu. Y: CANCEL this edit
   and restore its starting position/angle. B twice: request and confirm reset
   to default placement. You can still cancel the whole edit after a reset.
8. Menu: keep placement, save settings and close. Bot/machine stay OFF until
   you explicitly start a training mode.

The normal hold-Meta system recenter remains available. On a tracking-origin
update the panel is placed in front of you again; centre both sticks to resume.
If your headset/system recenter changes the room alignment, use this page again.

Placement is saved locally on this headset only. It is NOT included in shared
HP2 setup codes and does not replace room scanning or the Quest safety boundary.
Changing court sizes keeps your manual offset/angle while adjusting the default
near-back-wall player anchor. Re-check the fit against furniture after any size
change; a full-size 10 x 20 m court does not fit safely in a small room.

Sound controls
Court & physics > Impact sounds & volume. More sound options now includes only
glass style, rolling and menu clicks. Old saved Swing=ON cannot reactivate the
removed movement sound. Original downloaded sound files have not been deleted.

Before sharing: install over the old app, do not uninstall or clear app data if
you want to keep grip, physics settings and recorded reports. Real headset
readability, alignment and subjective feel still require a test in your room.
